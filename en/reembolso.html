Refund Policy

Please read carefully the deadlines, conditions, and the refund and subscription cancellation process.

Last updated: September 12, 2026

1. Refund Request Deadline

You can request a refund for your subscription within 7 (seven) days after the charge. After this period, refunds can no longer be processed.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact us through:

  • Email: ciadeenfermagem@gmail.com
  • Contact form: Contact Us

3. Refund Conditions

  • Refunds are granted only if you have not used the platform features after the subscription renewal.
  • Refunds are not granted for subscriptions canceled after the services have been used during the charged period.
  • In case of duplicate charges or a system error, the refund will be processed in full.

4. Refund Processing

  • After approval, the refund will be processed within 5 (five) business days.
  • The amount will be credited to the same payment method used for the purchase.
  • You will receive a confirmation email when the refund is processed.

5. Subscription Cancellation

  • You can request to cancel your subscription at any time through the Contact Us or by the official email ciadeenfermagem@gmail.com.
  • Cancellation prevents future charges but does not generate an automatic refund for the current period.
  • After cancellation, you will keep access to the features until the end of the already-paid period.

6. Important Notes

  • Stripe processing fees are non-refundable.
  • This policy is subject to change, and you will be notified in advance.

By subscribing to any plan, you declare that you have read and agreed to this Refund Policy and the Terms of Use.
