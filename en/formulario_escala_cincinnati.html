About this form

The Cincinnati Scale supports the rapid identification of signs suggestive of stroke during initial assessment.

  • Use the original document to record facial asymmetry, arm drift, and speech changes.
  • The PDF is presented in full, without conversion or alteration.
  • In case of clinical suspicion, follow local protocols and activate emergency care.
