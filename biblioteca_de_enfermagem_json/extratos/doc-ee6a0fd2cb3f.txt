# PRISMA 2020

PRISMA 2020 consists of a statement paper , which includes a description of how the reporting guideline was developed and presents a checklist of items and sub-items, an expanded checklist that details reporting recommendations for each item/sub-item, an abstract checklist , and template flow diagrams for original and updated reviews. Users are advised to read the PRISMA 2020 statement paper in conjunction with the explanation and elaboration paper , which provides exemplars of good reporting and explanations to enhance the use, understanding and dissemination of PRISMA 2020.
